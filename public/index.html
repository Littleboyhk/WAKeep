Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to set up a 5-minute ping or cron-job.org?

No, zero setup is required for website users! When you activate your session on this website (wakeep.onrender.com), the cloud server automatically runs a continuous 5-minute keep-alive ping that keeps all user sessions awake 24/7. The 5-minute cron ping instructions on GitHub are strictly for developers who fork the repository to self-host their own instance on Render's free tier.

Does my phone need to stay on the same Wi-Fi?

No. WhatsApp Multi-Device protocol operates independently. Your phone can be on mobile data or anywhere in the world.

What happens when Render restarts?

WAKeep reads the encrypted keys from your Supabase vault, decrypts them in memory, and immediately resumes the session. You never need to scan the QR code again.

Can other visitors see or access my WhatsApp session?

No. Every user session has its own unique name and is protected by your mandatory 8+ character password. Furthermore, session tokens in the database are encrypted using AES-256-GCM, and real-time WebSocket events are strictly scoped so other users cannot receive your QR code, connection events, or messages.

🛡️ Zero-Knowledge & Zero Data Collection Policy

This Privacy Policy governs the use of WAKeep, a self-hosted, open-source WhatsApp Web companion gateway.

1. Absolute Zero Data Collection Guarantee

The creators, developers, contributors, and open-source maintainers of WAKeep DO NOT collect, store, intercept, inspect, transmit, harvest, or monetize any of your data:

  • Zero Message Data: We never see, copy, or read your incoming or outgoing WhatsApp messages.
  • Zero Contact Harvesting: We do not collect contact lists, phone numbers, or chat metadata.
  • Zero Remote Analytics: There are no third-party tracking scripts, advertising trackers, or developer telemetry pings embedded in this software.
  • Zero Credential Access: Your authentication secrets and encryption keys are held strictly in your own environment.

2. Compliance with Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India)

In accordance with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act):

  • Data Principal: You (the user) are the sole Data Principal of your WhatsApp data.
  • Data Fiduciary: Because WAKeep is a self-hosted software deployed onto your own personal Supabase database and Render instance, you are the exclusive operator and Data Fiduciary of your personal instance.
  • No Third-Party Data Transfer: All cryptographic operations happen locally inside your deployment container. Data never leaves your private cloud boundary.

3. Client-Side Cryptographic Security

All multi-device session credentials and pre-keys are encrypted client-side using industry-standard AES-256-GCM (Authenticated Encryption) before being written to your personal Supabase database. Without your local 32-byte VAULT_SECRET_KEY, the database contents are mathematically undecipherable.
