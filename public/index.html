Engineered for 24/7 Continuity

How WAKeep keeps your companion session active without ever timing out.

01

Steady 25s Heartbeats

WhatsApp drops sessions when companion tabs sleep. WAKeep emits steady cryptographic pings every 25 seconds, keeping the NAT pipeline permanently warm.

02

AES-256-GCM Supabase Vault

Free cloud instances wipe their disks on restart. WAKeep encrypts your multi-device credentials before saving to Supabase PostgreSQL, restoring them instantly on reboot.

03

Zero Phone Silencing

Using passive companion mode (markOnlineOnConnect: false), your phone still rings and gets push notifications normally.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does my phone need to stay on the same Wi-Fi?

No. WhatsApp Multi-Device protocol operates independently. Your phone can be on mobile data or anywhere in the world.

What happens when Render restarts?

WAKeep reads the encrypted keys from your Supabase vault, decrypts them in memory, and immediately resumes the session. You never need to scan the QR code again.
